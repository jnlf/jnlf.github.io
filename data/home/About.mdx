Welcome! My name is Jocelyn La Fleur and I am a 5th year PhD Candidate at Yale School of the Environment.
My research incorporates tools and methods from energy economics, network economics, and computational economics. My research focus is in the adoption and diffusion of renewable energy technologies, with a primary focus in electric vehicles (EVs) and a secondary focus in solar photovolataics (PVs). Using tools from empirical game theory, natural language processing (NLP), and spatial econometrics, I investigate the barriers to achieving a widespread and sustainable national EV charging station network in the U.S. for both infrastructure builders and consumers and the current welfare consequences for both parties.
